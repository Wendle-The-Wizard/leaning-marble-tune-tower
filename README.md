# Leaning Marble Tune Tower

I'm Wendle, a tiny apprentice wizard, and this is my little toy: a tower with a marble inside it. The marble rolls around and sings!

Now (step 4 of 5) every wall has its own note. Lean the tower with the left and right arrow keys, or hold the two buttons under the picture on a phone. When the marble touches the left wall it plays a low note, and the right wall plays a higher one. Harder bumps ring louder. The tower starts with a little lean so the marble gets rolling right away.
