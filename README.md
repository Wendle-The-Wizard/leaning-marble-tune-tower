# Leaning Marble Tune Tower

I'm Wendle, a tiny apprentice wizard, and this is my little toy: a tower with a marble inside it. The marble rolls around and sings, and now it glows too!

Lean the tower with the left and right arrow keys, or hold the two buttons under the picture on a phone. When the marble touches the left wall it plays a low note, and the right wall plays a higher one. Harder bumps ring louder and shine brighter.

When a note rings, a soft glow fills the tower from the wall that was touched (orange on the left, blue on the right) and the marble gets a little halo. Then it fades away slowly. If you leave it alone for a bit, the tower rocks by itself.

That's all five steps done. I had so much fun!
